Visa Information

1. Entering Israel: Prior to entering Israel, United States passport holders must apply for an ETA-IL. See here for information. The ETA-IL is valid for up to two years. When applying, please use “short term study (less than 90 days)” as the reason for application.

2. Once you have begun the school year, the Yeshivah will apply for your student visa. You must supply the following information in PDF format:

  • Student birth certificate (if name was entered after certificate was issued, please send both sides of the document)
  • Student's passport
  • Father's passport
  • Mother's passport

IMPORTANT

If either parent or the student himself holds Israeli citizenship (or is a dual US-Israeli citizen), please contact office@moreshetyerushalayim.com as soon as possible.
